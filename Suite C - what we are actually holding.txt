CAIRNWELL TECHNOLOGIES
Suite C -- what we are actually holding

Prepared by:  Marcus Reyes
Date:         15 September 2014
Status:       Internal

Six months in.  Alice's January document said somebody would want to know
whether this was stupid or unlucky.  It is early to answer that and I am going
to write down where we are instead, because I would rather do it now than in
December when it will be tidier and less true.


THE MONEY

    Committed floor          180 kW, payable regardless
    Drawing today            141 kW
    Billing today            152 kW of customer commitment

So we are covering the floor on paper and under it in reality, and the gap
between 152 and 300 is the thing we are carrying.

Cash out of pocket against the floor: about four thousand a month at the
moment.  Alice's January worst case was eleven.  We are inside her number,
which is the first thing to say and I want it said before the rest of this.


WHAT HAPPENED TO THE CUSTOMERS

**Halvard.**  Fine.  Better than fine.  They moved to ASIC hardware in the
spring and their draw went up, not down.  Petra renegotiated nothing and pays
on time.  They are 96 of our 141.

**Crestone.**  This is the one that did not happen.  They contracted 60 kW on
two years and took 24.  Their expansion depended on hardware they ordered in
February from a vendor who took the money and has delivered, as of this week,
about a third of it, eight months late.  They are not unusual.  Everybody I
talk to has a story about a preorder.

    They are paying for 60 and using 24.  That is good for us this year and
    it is a customer with a grievance, which is not good for us next year.

**Vail Hashworks.**  Paying, using about 21 of 40.  Quieter than they were.

**Lindqvist.**  Gary's GPUs stopped being worth running for Bitcoin.  That has
been true for a while and got true faster this year.  He moved them to mining
something else in March, which worked until about June, and last month he
asked to reduce from 9 kW to 3.

    I said yes.  He has been our customer since before the suite existed and
    the three kilowatts costs us nothing we are not already paying for.

    He also asked whether we would buy his rigs.  We said no.


THE PART I WANT WRITTEN DOWN PROPERLY

The suite is not empty.  The suite is the wrong shape, and it is the wrong
shape in exactly the way I said it might be in October last year.

I designed for 12 kW a rack when we needed 8.  I wrote at the time that this
buys one generation and not two, and I meant it as a limit rather than a
worry.

The ASIC machines Halvard runs want 17 to 20 kW a rack.  They are not bigger
machines; they are the same size and they eat more.  So Halvard's 96 kW sits
in eleven racks that should be six, and we have floor we cannot fill with
power and power we cannot fill with floor.

    Racks in the suite         44
    Racks occupied             31
    Power drawn                141 kW
    Power we could draw if
    every rack were ASIC-dense  around 200 kW, and we would need PDU and
                                distribution work to get there

None of this is a disaster.  It means the 300 kW ceiling is theoretical for
the hardware people now buy, and our practical ceiling is closer to 200 unless
we spend money.  We have not lost the power.  We cannot currently sell it.


WHAT I AM NOT GOING TO SAY

That we should have known.

I raised density in October, Alice recorded in January that I was more right
than she was, and none of that means any of us knew what was coming.  What I
said in October was that if machines got denser we would have built the wrong
room.  I did not know whether they would.  I thought it was worth designing
against and we designed against it to the tune of one generation, which is
what we could afford.

If I had asked for 20 kW a rack in February 2014 I would have been asking for
a lot of money against a thing nobody could demonstrate, and Alice would have
been right to ask me what evidence I had, and I did not have any.

**We were not wrong. We were early, and we bought one generation of headroom
when two would have been better and nobody could have justified two.**

That is a different sentence from "we should have known" and I want the
difference in the file.


WHAT I THINK WE DO

Nothing dramatic this quarter.

    Find out what the distribution work costs to take a block of the suite to
    20 kW a rack.  Price it, do not do it.  Same as I did with space and power
    in March 2013, and that is the only reason we could answer Petra in two
    days.

    Talk to Crestone before their renewal and not at it.  They have a
    grievance that is not with us and they will bring it to us anyway.

    Stop selling on the 300 number.  Our practical capacity is about 200 for
    what people are buying and I would rather quote what we can deliver.

    Fill the floor we have with something that is not mining.  We have 13
    empty racks in a compliant suite in a facility people want to be in.  That
    was the whole argument in January.  Alice said somebody would want this
    room.  Nobody has asked yet, and nobody has been asked.
