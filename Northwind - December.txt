NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES
December

Prepared by:  John Mercer
Date:         9 December 2013
Status:       Internal


WHAT HAS HAPPENED

The price was about a hundred and thirty when I wrote the September note.  It
was around two hundred at the start of November.  It is above a thousand today
and it was twelve hundred last week.

I am not going to pretend I understand why.  Nobody I have spoken to
understands why either, including the people it has made rich.

What I can describe is what it has done to my telephone.

    Since 1 November: thirty-one enquiries.  In the three months before
    that: nine.

    Petra wants the other seventy kilowatts and she wants it in the first
    quarter.  She has offered three years.  She has never offered anybody
    three years.

    Two of the three serious ones from the autumn have come back with money
    and dates.  One of them asked what our deposit terms are before I had
    quoted him anything.

    Four people have asked whether we will build to suit.  I did not know
    what to say to that in September.  I still do not, but it is now four
    people.

The cage is 39 of 40 kilowatts.  We have no room.  I have been telling people
no for five weeks with a facility we signed three weeks ago.


WHAT I WANT

Three hundred kilowatts.

Alder Street will do a suite — their own words, "we would build it for you" —
on three years, with the electrical work as a capital contribution we amortise
or pay for outright.  Marcus has the drawings on his desk.

Against that I have:

    Halvard              100 kW, three years, signed subject to delivery
    Crestone Compute     60 kW, two years, deposit offered
    Two others           together about 70 kW, two years, verbal
    Lindqvist             9 kW, rolling

That is 239 of 300 committed or near it, at terms longer than the lease.

I want to be clear that this is the same rule Alice made me agree to in
November and not a departure from it.  I am not asking to build ahead of the
customers.  The customers arrived.


WHAT I AM NOT SAYING

That the price stays here.  I have no view on the price and I would not trust
mine if I had one.

What I would say is that the price does not have to stay here for this to work.
These people have already bought their hardware.  The money is spent.  They
need somewhere to put it whether the price is a thousand or three hundred,
because the alternative is that it sits in a garage earning nothing.

If it goes to thirty, all bets are off and I know that.


WHAT I AM ASKING ALICE FOR

Not permission.  We are partners and she can say no and it does not happen.

I am asking her to tell me what has to be true for this to be a reasonable
decision, and then I will go and find out whether those things are true, and
we will decide in January and not in the week Bitcoin did whatever it did.
