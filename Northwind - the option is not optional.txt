NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES
The option is not optional

Prepared by:  Alice Chen
Date:         19 March 2019
Status:       Internal
Read by:      John, Marcus, Priya, Wes, Toby


I asked Priya what was behind the floor.  The answer is nothing, and I have
spent three weeks being wrong about what that means.


WHAT I THOUGHT THE DECISION WAS

Alder Street want five years at a hundred and thirty-six a kilowatt with three
per cent a year.  A hundred and eighty kilowatts, payable whether we sell it or
not.  About three hundred thousand a year and about one and a half million over
the term.

Priya's census says we have nothing committed past 31 March 2020.  Halvard
expires on 29 February, one month before the new term would start.  Crestone
are monthly.  Sightline is undated.  Dr Ma's grant ended in September and
nobody asked her what happens next.  The managed four are thirty days.

So the decision looked like: do we commit a million and a half against nothing.
And the rule says no.  We do not commit longer than the commitment behind it.

I was going to write that memo.  I had most of it.


WHAT THE DECISION ACTUALLY IS

Marcus asked me what we do on 1 April 2020 if we do not exercise.

We move.

    Halvard's ninety-four kilowatts.  Eleven racks of ASIC hardware belonging
    to somebody who signed with us because we could hold power.

    Crestone's twenty-three.  Sightline's twelve machines.  Dr Ma's fourteen
    nodes, which are ours.  Our own four racks, including mon1, which is the
    machine every customer's monitoring runs on.

    Twenty-nine racks.  A hundred and forty-seven kilowatts.  A room we spent
    ninety-four thousand dollars electrifying in 2014, which stays behind.

    With six people, one of whom knows how a room works, in twelve months,
    while continuing to run everything.

There is space in Denver.  Renata as good as told us so when she quoted us
below what we pay.  Somebody would take us.

    That is not the question.  The question is whether this company can
    execute a move of that size without dropping something, and I do not think
    it can, and I do not think anybody here thinks it can.

So the five-year option is not a thing we are choosing to take.  It is a thing
we will take because the alternative is worse, and I would rather write that
down in plain words than dress it up as a strategy.

    I signed a three-year renewal in January 2017 to avoid committing to five.
    What I actually did was buy twenty-six months of the same decision and
    make it in a worse position, because the room now has a hundred and
    forty-seven kilowatts in it instead of eighty-eight.

    The rule protects you from commitments you can walk away from.  It has
    nothing to say about the one you cannot.

That is the thing I did not understand about term matching and have not
understood since 2013.


WHAT MARCUS SAID, WHICH I AM NOT GOING TO SOFTEN

He does not accept that we cannot move.

    "It is a hundred and forty-seven kilowatts and twenty-nine racks.  I have
    moved more than that twice.  It would be six months of my life and it
    would cost us a customer, probably Crestone, and I would want Toby full
    time on it and somebody covering him.  It is not impossible.  It is
    expensive and horrible and I would want to be paid for it."

I asked him whether he would recommend it.

    "No.  But you asked whether we can, and we can, and I do not want the
    answer written down as we cannot when what we mean is we would rather
    not.  Those are different and the second one is honest."

He is right and I have changed the sentence above.  We would rather not.


WHAT I AM DECIDING NOW

**One.  We are going to exercise, unless something changes.**

I am not signing anything in March.  Notice is due 30 September and I want the
months.  But I am telling everybody now what I expect to do, because Fourmile
need to know, Halvard's renewal needs to be negotiated by somebody who knows,
and I do not want John selling against a decision I have not told him about.

**Two.  Petra first, and now.**

Halvard expires 29 February 2020.  Ninety-four of our hundred and forty-seven
kilowatts.  Nobody has spoken to her about renewal.

    In September 2014 Marcus wrote: "Talk to Crestone before their renewal and
    not at it.  They have a grievance that is not with us and they will bring
    it to us anyway."

    We wrote that down, we did not do it with Crestone, and here we are four
    and a half years later about to not do it with Halvard.

John goes to Petra in April.  Not to renew -- to ask what she wants, a year
out, while we still have something to offer her.

    What I want to know is whether she intends to be in that room in 2021.  I
    do not need a signature in April.  I need to not be surprised in December.

**Three.  Dr Ma, this month.**

Her funding ended in September.  She has paid five months against nothing and
nobody has asked.  That is not a commercial problem yet and it is exactly the
kind of thing that becomes one.  Priya is doing it.

**Four.  Crestone, before June.**

John is to find the 2015 settlement or establish that there is not one.
Twenty-three kilowatts on terms nobody can produce.

**Five.  We are not spending the eighty-two thousand.**

Unchanged from December.  Fourmile is the only reason to spend it and Fourmile
have told us what they need and it is not fifty kilowatts for sixteen months.

    If we exercise in September and Fourmile are still interested, we spend it
    then, against their signature, which is the rule and has been since 2013.


FOURMILE

Dinesh's letter is the best thing that has happened to us this year and it does
not contain a sale.

    "Come back to us when you know about 2020... we would not go out to the
    market first.  We are not going to wait past then."

We told them a smaller true number instead of a larger one we could not
deliver, and they have given us until the autumn and first refusal.  That is
what honesty bought and it is worth more than sixteen months of revenue.

It also means our September decision has a customer attached to it, which is
either useful discipline or pressure I have just invited onto myself.  I think
the first.  John thinks I am fooling myself and has said so.


WHAT I AM NOT DOING

Looking at other buildings.  Marcus's point stands -- we can move -- and I am
not going to spend his spring proving it while there is a renewal to negotiate
and a customer waiting.  If Petra says she is leaving, that changes.

Hiring.  Raising money.  A second suite.  Buying anything.

And configuration management, which Wes has written up separately.  I asked him
to look at it because I noticed I was about to say no for the third time and
could not remember what my reasons had been.  His first draft said it could not
run on our machines and I would have signed that.  His second says it can, and
that the question is what it would be for.  Where he has landed -- nothing we
run for other people, everything we build for ourselves, once somebody has
signed for it -- is not a no, and it is not something I am doing before
September either.  It is on the list with his name on it.


WHAT I HAVE NOT SOLVED

Marcus.  Sixth time.

If we sign five years we are saying that one man's knowledge of one room is
load-bearing until 2025.  He has said so, I have agreed each time, and the
agreeing has become a ritual.

    I am not going to pretend this memo addresses it.  It does not.  It is on
    the deferred list with my name on it and a date, and that was true in 2014
    and 2017 and it is true now, and at some point writing it down stops being
    honesty and starts being an alibi.

    -- AC
