CAIRNWELL TECHNOLOGIES
Following the instructions exactly

Prepared by:  Wes Okafor
Date:         2 November 2012
Status:       Internal

Priya asked me to work through the alerts file and the customer records and do
exactly what they say, and to write down everywhere that doing exactly what they
say does not work.  She said it was the most useful thing a new person can do
and that it stops being possible after about six weeks.

I think she meant I would stop noticing.  This is week seven so I may already
have.


THINGS THE INSTRUCTIONS SAY THAT ARE NOT TRUE

1.  alerts, under disk, says "Logs and old backups are usually it.  Rotate or
    remove."  On ccdata1 and ccdata2 the thing that fills up is /data, which is
    the array, and you must not remove anything from it.  The Clear Creek
    record says this.  The alerts file does not, and the alerts file is the one
    you read at 2am.

2.  alerts, under backup, says the check "cannot tell whether the dump is any
    good.  Only a restore does that."  True.  It does not say how to do a
    restore.  I asked Priya and she talked me through it in about four minutes.
    It is not written down anywhere.

3.  The Summit record says to telephone Laura out of hours.  It gives her
    mobile.  It does not say that you should not telephone Mark, and I did,
    because he is the developer and it looked like an application problem.  He
    was polite about it.  Priya said afterwards that we never telephone the
    customer's developer directly, we telephone the customer.  That is not
    written anywhere I could find.

4.  Three of the records say "same as everybody" under ROUTINE.  I did not know
    what everybody was.  It is in the README and I had not read the README
    because nothing told me to.

5.  cwpush -n on brightwater prints DIFFERS for cw-cleanup on bw-web1.  I
    thought I had broken something.  It is because Brightwater's copy was
    pushed in July and somebody edited it on the machine in August.  Nobody
    knows who.  I have left it alone and told Priya.


THINGS I GOT WRONG THAT WERE MY FAULT

I restarted Apache on hr-www at 11 in the morning because the disk check had
fired and I wanted a clean log.  Nothing broke.  Priya pointed out that if
anything had, it would have been me, in business hours, on a customer's
production web server, for tidiness.

She was not cross about it.  She said the rule is that you do not restart a
thing to make a symptom go away, and that the rule is not written down either.


WHAT I THINK THIS MEANS

Most of what stopped me was not missing documentation.  It was documentation
that was correct for the person who wrote it.

The alerts file is Priya's, and everything she knows without thinking is
missing from it, because she does not know she knows it.  The records are
Alice's and the same is true.

I do not think the answer is more files.  I think somebody who has just arrived
should read them and write down what does not work, and that person is me until
about Christmas.
