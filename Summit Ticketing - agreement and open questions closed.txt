Summit Ticketing -- agreement signed, open questions answered

Prepared by:  John Mercer
Date:         May 26, 2010
Status:       Internal

Telephone call with Laura on the 19th, agreement signed today, monthly service
starts June 1. Writing down what I actually told her so that Alice knows what
we are now on the hook for and so that I remember it myself.


WHAT I TOLD HER ABOUT AFTER HOURS

She asked what happens at eleven o'clock on a Friday night in July. I gave her
the true answer rather than a clause.

The number rings Alice's mobile. There is no rota and there is no second
person. If Alice is asleep the phone wakes her. If she is somewhere without
signal, it does not, and Laura will not know the difference until Alice calls
back.

What we will do is call back and tell her what we know, including when what we
know is nothing yet.

What we will not do is promise that someone is awake and looking within any
number of minutes, because I would be inventing the number.

I also told her the part I did not have to. When we take more customers this
arrangement stops working, and we will tell her when we change it rather than
letting her find out during an incident.

She said she would rather have that than a better-sounding promise from a
company that would not keep it. I think she meant it, and I also think she will
feel differently at eleven o'clock on a Friday night in July. Both can be true.


WHAT I TOLD HER ABOUT A THIRD SERVER

The honest answer is that we do not have a pricing rule and I said so.

What I told her we would do: work out what it costs us to watch and maintain
the machine, and add that. For another web server much like WEB2, in the region
of two hundred a month. A database server would be more, because it is more
work and more risk, and I would want Alice to look at it before I quoted
anything.

She said that was a more useful answer than a number would have been. I am not
sure she is right, but it was the answer I had.

This is now the second customer conversation where the lack of a pricing rule
has cost me something. It has not cost us a deal yet. It will.


THE AGREEMENT

Signed today. Her attorney read it. Three changes:

    Thirty days' notice either way rather than sixty. She wanted to be able to
    leave. I would rather she can leave and does not.

    Liability capped at three months of fees. Their suggestion, and reasonable
    for what we are charging.

    The clause about Mark's undisclosed changes being billable is in, but she
    asked that we tell her before we bill for it rather than after. That is
    fair and I should have offered it.

Nothing in it contradicts what Alice and I agreed in April.


WHERE WE ARE

Summit is a customer. Monthly service starts June 1 at $1,200. The initial
engagement is invoiced and paid.

Alice's list of things she would fix if asked is still a list of things nobody
has asked her to fix. I have not raised it with Laura because none of it is
urgent and I did not want the first month of a monthly agreement to open with
an invoice for extra work.

I am aware that is a reason and not a good one.
